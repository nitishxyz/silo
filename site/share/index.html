Someone shared a Silo session with you.

The link opens a live session, or one pane of it, in the Silo app. Anyone who has it can open the session until it expires or is revoked, so keep it private. This page reads the link in your browser and sends it nowhere.

Open in Silo

If nothing opens

Silo 0.1.33 and older do not open links from a web page.

  1. Copy the link.
  2. In Silo, press ⌘P and choose Open Shared Link…
  3. Paste the link into Ticket and click Pair.

Get Silo

Shared links open in the Silo app for macOS (Apple silicon): download it from Releases. To run a Silo server of your own on Linux or macOS:
